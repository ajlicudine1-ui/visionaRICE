Before You Scan

Before capturing or uploading a rice leaf image, follow these best practices to improve detection accuracy. Proper image quality allows VisionaRice to analyze the leaf more effectively and provide more reliable results.

🔍
Ensure Good Lighting Take the photo in bright natural light for clearer disease detection.
📋
Focus on the Affected Area Keep the damaged area clear and in focus.
🌐
Avoid Blurry Images Hold your phone steady to capture a sharp image.
🌐
Take the Photo at the Right Distance Keep enough distance to fit the entire leaf while showing disease details.
