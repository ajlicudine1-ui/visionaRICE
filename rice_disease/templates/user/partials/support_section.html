Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the disease detection?
Our AI model has been trained on thousands of rice disease images and achieves over 95% accuracy in detecting common rice diseases. However, we always recommend consulting with agricultural experts for critical decisions.
What diseases can the app detect?
The app can detect major rice diseases including Bacterial Leaf Blight, Brown Spot, Leaf Blast, and can also identify healthy rice plants. We continuously update our database with new diseases.
How do I take a good image?
For best results: 1) Take photos in good natural lighting, 2) Get close to the affected leaf, 3) Ensure the image is clear and in focus, 4) Capture the diseased area clearly, and 5) Avoid shadows and reflections. The app will guide you through the process.
Can I use it without a signal?
Currently, an internet connection is required for disease detection as our AI model runs on cloud servers. However, you can view your previous scan history offline. We're working on an offline mode for future releases.
What does the prediction result mean?
Each detection result shows the suspected disease and confidence level, the top three possible diseases, recommended actions to manage the disease, and the location where it was detected.
Is my data secure and private?
Yes! We take data privacy seriously. Your images and scan results are encrypted and stored securely. We never share your personal data with third parties without your explicit consent. You can delete your data anytime from the settings.
